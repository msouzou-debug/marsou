# The eCapital mark

Chosen by the owner on 13/11/2026 from three directions (direction C,
«Γερανός»). The e of eCapital, its bar extended as a tower-crane jib with a
hook: capital works in progress. White on the ΟΚΥπΥ blue gradient, so the
tool reads as one of the family with eMAP, eFinance and eArchive, and a
shape of its own: no sprout, no cross.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `logo/ecapital-mark.svg` | the mark, colour, any size |
| `logo/ecapital-mark-mono.svg` | black, for print, stamps, fax |
| `logo/ecapital-mark-reverse.svg` | white tile with blue shapes, on a blue or dark ground |
| `logo/ecapital-lockup.svg` | mark and wordmark side by side (text in Lato Black; open in a browser or Inkscape with Lato installed) |
| `logo/ecapital-mark-*.png` | 64, 128, 512 px for Word, PowerPoint and e-mail signatures |

In the app: `apps/web/src/components/app-shell/EcapitalMark.tsx` draws it
inline (top bar at 28 px, sign-in at 56 px) and `apps/web/src/app/icon.svg`
is the browser tab icon.

Rules

- Grid 64 × 64, corners 14. Minimum size 16 px on screen, 6 mm in print.
- Clear space around it of one quarter of its width.
- Colours from `brand-spec.md`: deep blue `#1B75BB` to bright blue
  `#069FEC`, 135°. Wordmark: «e» `#069FEC`, «Capital» `#1B75BB`, Lato Black,
  letter-spacing −2%.
- Do not recolour, rotate, outline or add a shadow. Do not put it inside the
  ΟΚΥπΥ sprout or next to it at the same size: the ΟΚΥπΥ logo is the
  organisation's, this one is the tool's.
- Official ΟΚΥπΥ documents the app prints (the shutdown permit, the asset
  labels) keep the ΟΚΥπΥ logo. The eCapital mark is for the application
  itself, its guides and its communications.
