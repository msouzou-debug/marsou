# Release 2026-10-11/12 — paging, single status filter, switcher stays put, eArchive ZIP, document preview

Date: 09/10/2026 (first two items), 11/10/2026 (ZIP), 12/10/2026 (preview)
Operator: Marios on the server (`ecapital-release-on-server --ref claude/ecstatic-cray-g0zhkx`)
Migrations: none. Seed: none.

## What these releases bring

- **Status filter** on Εντολές εργασίας and Εκκρεμότητες picks one status at
  a time; pressing the active one again shows all (owner, 09/10/2026).
- **Unit switcher** stays on the screen you are on and refilters it, instead
  of jumping to the project list (owner, 09/10/2026).
- **Εντολές εργασίας pages** 50 rows at a time, newest call first, with the
  counter «51–100 από 109» and two arrows under the table.
- **eArchive accepts ZIP** (eArchive build of 11/10/2026, ADR-0023 addendum):
  `application/zip` joins the whitelist on every document upload; eArchive
  opens the archive and files what is inside. Its refusals are 4xx and the
  outbox already treats them as final. eArchive's own build was deployed by
  Marios the same day with the three lines of their note (`/opt/emetroon`,
  `migrations: 20`, no database change).

- **Document preview** (owner, 12/10/2026): `GET /documents/:id/content`
  serves the operational copy through the caller's transaction, so the
  row policy decides who sees it. Filed photos show as thumbnails on the
  work order and the phone screen; PDFs, zips and office files open in a
  new tab with «Άνοιγμα», on work orders and on asset documents. HTML
  downloads instead of opening inline; a document eArchive deleted
  answers 404.
