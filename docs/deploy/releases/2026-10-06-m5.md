## What this release brings

Milestone M5, built from the Nicosia General Hospital electromechanical
maintenance contract (Α.Ο 42/24) and the owner's answers of 06/10/2026
(ADR-0031).

- **«Συντήρηση» is a working area**, not a placeholder: work orders with
  the contract's three timers (response, restore, written report, all
  counted from the call), preventive programme with an hourly generator,
  failure / cause / remedy codes, the maintenance backlog by risk band
  with «Σε έργο», and the contractor scorecard with its penalties.
- **Seven screens**: Εντολές εργασίας (S18), the order itself (S18a),
  Εκτέλεση εντολής on a phone (S19), Καταγραφή κλήσης (S20),
  Προληπτική συντήρηση with the SLA catalogue, the Excel importer and the
  programme (S18b), Εκκρεμότητες συντήρησης (S21), Αξιολόγηση αναδόχων
  (S22). Each with its help page, in both languages, and in the guides.
- **The Nicosia catalogue** is seeded from the contract: 48 systems with
  their bands and hours. **The penalty amounts per system are empty**
  until the owner confirms them; the scorecard says «ρήτρες ελλιπείς»
  and counts availability penalties only (5 €/h critical, 1 €/h other).
- **Excel exports** for the backlog and the scorecard with live formulas.
- UAT scenarios gain a maintenance section.
