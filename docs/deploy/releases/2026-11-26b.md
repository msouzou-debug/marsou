## What this release brings (owner decision of 26/11/2026)

Three weak points found while answering eHire's questions on users and roles:

- **Switched-off accounts and lost roles.** A permit line now goes only to
  somebody whose account is on and who holds a role allowed for that line.
  When the administrator switches an account off, or removes the role, that
  person's waiting lines move at once to the next eligible holder, or to
  nobody (the administrator can then decide them). The person's appointments
  stay on record and come back if the account is switched on again. The
  handback acceptance follows the same rule. Each move is in the permit's
  audit log; the permit's timeline does not show it.
- **The administrator and assigned lines.** The administrator can no longer
  decide a line assigned to somebody else. When they decide an unassigned
  line, the line names them.
- **One «Αποθήκευση» on the user sheet.** Roles, units, active and «Χώροι και
  ρόλοι έγκρισης» save together, so a new role and its appointment go in one
  save, and a refusal saves nothing. Saving without changes no longer fills
  the audit log with delete and insert rows for the appointments.

0036 adds one database function that answers, by id only, whether a person
is active and holds the given roles. No policy or grant changes.
