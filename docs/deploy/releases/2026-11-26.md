## What this release brings (owner decisions and UAT of 26/11/2026)

- **New role «Εγκρίνων τεχνικών και ασφάλειας»** (ADR-0039). It decides the
  Technical Services and Safety lines of a permit in the units it is
  appointed for, and no clinical line. New row «Έγκριση τεχνικών και
  ασφάλειας» in «Ρόλοι και δικαιώματα»: the new role, the Προϊστάμενος
  Τεχνικών Υπηρεσιών and the Διαχειριστής decide by default. «Χώροι και ρόλοι
  έγκρισης» on the user's sheet offers only what each role allows. Who starts
  works is unchanged.
- **RFI, same screen**: the letter or e-mail is attached on the new RFI, with
  «Αποθήκευση» or «Αποθήκευση και αποστολή e-mail». The second also records
  today as the sent day and downloads `RFI-<number>.eml`, which classic Outlook
  opens as a new message with recipient, subject, text and the attachment.
  «Απάντηση» takes an optional «Επιστολή ή e-mail αναδόχου».
- **Site instructions**: the same facility. Recipient and e-mail filled from
  the contractor's contact, sent day, filed correspondence, both buttons,
  `SI-<number>.eml`. No answer form.
- **Greek file names upload**: a name such as «Επιστολή προς ανάδοχο.pdf» was
  refused on every document upload. Fixed for all upload screens.
- **Permit close in two steps** (a fault UAT found: nobody could close a
  permit). The works side presses «Ολοκλήρωση εργασιών», ticks the five
  closeout boxes and «Υποβολή για αποδοχή»; the banner reads «Αναμονή
  αποδοχής παράδοσης» and the overrun clock stops. The clinical owner gets
  «Παράδοση χώρου για αποδοχή» in «Εγκρίσεις» and presses «Αποδοχή και
  κλείσιμο», or «Επιστροφή με σχόλια» (comment required), which goes back to
  the submitter's inbox. The administrator and the submitter cannot accept.
  A unit-wide Νοσηλευτική υπηρεσία or Έλεγχος λοιμώξεων officer can now open
  a permit of their unit while its handback waits for them (one narrow read
  addition in 0035; nothing else widens).
- **Finance «Επεξεργασία» on the project** from the approved phase on: a form
  with the approved budget only. Any other field from finance is refused.
- **«Απόρριψη»** on a permit shows only where the API allows it.
