# Release 2026-11-26 — a role of its own for the technical and safety approvers

Date: 26/11/2026
Operator: Marios on the server (`ecapital-release-on-server --ref claude/ecstatic-cray-g0zhkx`)
Migrations: 0033 `technical_approver_role`, applied by the release script. Seed: none.

## What this release brings (owner decision of 26/11/2026, ADR-0039)

- **New role «Εγκρίνων τεχνικών και ασφάλειας»** (Technical and safety
  approver). It decides the Technical Services and Safety lines of a permit
  in the units it is appointed for, and no clinical line. Estates staff who
  sign those lines no longer need the «Κλινικός εγκριτής» role, and so no
  longer get clinical approval rights.
- **New row in «Ρόλοι και δικαιώματα»**: «Έγκριση τεχνικών και ασφάλειας».
  By default the new role, the Προϊστάμενος Τεχνικών Υπηρεσιών and the
  Διαχειριστής decide; everybody else sees. For every other row the new role
  starts with what the Κλινικός εγκριτής has, except «Κλινική έγκριση», which
  it only sees.
- **«Χώροι και ρόλοι έγκρισης»** on the user's sheet now opens for the new
  role, the Κλινικός εγκριτής and the Προϊστάμενος Τεχνικών Υπηρεσιών, and
  offers only what the role allows: Technical Services and Safety for the
  new role and the estates head, everything for the clinical approver.
- **Unchanged**: who may start works and close a permit
  («Έναρξη εργασιών και κλείσιμο»).

## For the owner

Clinical approvers who hold Technical Services or Safety lines today keep
deciding them; nothing moves on its own. When convenient, the administrator
moves each of them to the new role: open the user in «Χρήστες», tick
«Εγκρίνων τεχνικών και ασφάλειας», untick «Κλινικός εγκριτής» if the person
decides nothing clinical, save, and check that the unit's Technical Services
and Safety lines are still in «Χώροι και ρόλοι έγκρισης».
