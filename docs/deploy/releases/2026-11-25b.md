## What this release brings (owner's checks after the first release of 25/11/2026)

- **RFI**: «Απάντηση» and «Κλείσιμο αιτήματος» in the open RFI. Closing
  without an answer needs a closing note. Who closed it, when and the note
  are kept.
- **Contractor contact**: contact person, e-mail and telephone on the
  contractor, edited on the contractors screen. A new RFI fills the
  recipient and e-mail from it; both stay editable. eFinance carries no
  e-mail or telephone today; the request to add them is
  `docs/integration/2026-11-25-to-efinance-vendor-contact-fields.md`. When
  eFinance sends them, eCapital fills only empty contacts and never
  overwrites one typed by a person. No bank details are stored.
- **Asset condition**: the dropdown starts on the asset's condition and stays
  on the saved value, with «Η φυσική κατάσταση καταγράφηκε.». The change
  itself was being saved; the dropdown jumping back to «A» made it look lost.
- **ICRA stuck at acknowledgement**: the checklist box was cut at a fixed
  height, so on Class III–V only five controls could be ticked and
  «Συνέχεια» never enabled. It now scrolls and counts «x από y».
- **Permit edit**: «Επεξεργασία» on a draft or returned permit. Changing the
  rooms or the work kind asks for the ICRA again. An approved permit is a
  new request.
- **Approvals**: the head of estates could not decide the technical and
  safety lines routed to them (only the clinical approver role could), and
  the screen hid the refusal. Fixed. «Επιστροφή με σχόλια» needs a comment
  and sends the permit to the requester's inbox. Lines with nobody appointed
  say so, and the administrator can decide them.
