## What this release brings (owner's UAT list of 25/11/2026)

- **Excel export works on every table** (points 1, 2, 5, 6, 11). Frozen bold
  header, filters, € and dd/mm/yyyy formats, live `SUBTOTAL` totals, a
  «Πληροφορίες» sheet. Projects exports every project matching the filters.
- **History collapsed** behind «Εμφάνιση ιστορικού (N)» on every screen (3, 21).
- **Project cash-flow plan**, editable by month, with Excel template and
  upload (4, ADR-0037). Edited by finance and the administrator.
- **RFIs, site instructions, defects** open on click and have
  «Επεξεργασία» (7, 24, 25, 26). A click anywhere on a row opens it in every list.
- **RFI recipient, sent-on date and filed correspondence** (.msg/.eml/.pdf) (8).
  eCapital sends no e-mail.
- **Claims**: date notified not in the future; claim letter, reply and
  lawyer's letter filed on the claim (9).
- **Dates in order across the tool** (9, 12, 19): nothing that has happened
  can be in the future; no end before its start.
- **Assets**: condition saves (10, 16); a refused asset names its field (14);
  full source project list (13); View and Download for documents (17);
  labels fit when printed (20); SAP asset class on every asset with a filter (18).
- **Contracts**: filters by unit and project (15).
- **Maintenance agreements dashboard** with unit, equipment type,
  contractor and expiry filters (22).
- **VAT** (23, ADR-0038): contract, variation and certificate amounts are
  net plus a rate; screens and Excel show net, VAT and gross; budgets and all
  budget comparisons are gross. **Existing amounts are taken as net at 19%:
  committed totals rise by 19% on screen.** SAP actuals are taken as gross
  until finance confirms.
