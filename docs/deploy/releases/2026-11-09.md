## What this release brings

The six contract-level findings of the ΜΕΕ report of November 2026
(`docs/audit/2026-11-09-MEE-capital-findings-vs-eCapital.md`), owner
decision of 09/11/2026: build all six, **the penalty is shown, finance
deducts it.**

- **«Προθεσμίες και ρήτρες»**, a new tab on every contract (S07h): one
  delay clock per section, or the contract's own; days late from the due
  date to the handover or to today; the accrued «ρήτρα καθυστέρησης» at
  the contract's daily rate; «Ενεργοποίηση ρήτρας» for the head of estates
  and the administrator. Nothing is deducted: the payment certificate form
  shows the figure as a notice to finance.
- **Sections** («Τμήμα Α», «Τμήμα Β») with their own completion and
  handover dates and an optional rate.
- **Extensions of time** as records: days, date, approver (ΤΕΑΑ, ΚΕΑΑ,
  engineer, other), name, reason, contractor culpable yes or no. The
  contract form no longer takes a number; the total is the sum of the
  records, kept by the database. Existing values were carried over as one
  record marked «Μεταφορά».
- **«Αξιώσεις»** (S07i): claims by the contractor or by the Organisation,
  amount, stage, legal advisor, contingent-liability flag for finance.
- **Freshness**: «Ενημέρωση» column on the project list; a project nobody
  touched for 30 days is a row in the exceptions report.
- **Business case gate**: a project cannot leave «Ιδέα» without a filed
  business case.
- Exceptions report and contractor scorecard carry the penalty, the open
  claims, the stale days and the culpable extension days, in Excel too.
- Default role matrix: `contractRecords` APPROVE for admin and head of
  estates (a cell the administrator had already changed is left alone).
