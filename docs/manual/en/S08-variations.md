# Contract variations

This screen records every change to a contract's value or time, from proposal to decision. Project engineers use it to raise variations, and the head of estates or the system administrator use it to decide on them.

## Steps

1. Press "Add" for a new variation, or open an existing row from the list.
2. While it is a draft, fill in the description, reason, value and time impact, and press "Save".
3. Press "Submit" once it is ready for a decision. You cannot change it after that.
4. The approver sees the value, the reason and the new current value, and picks "Approve", "Return with comments" or "Reject".
5. An approved variation adds its value to the contract's current value; a returned or rejected one does not.

## What can go wrong

- **You do not see the decision buttons, or they are disabled.** You cannot approve a variation you raised yourself; the screen shows this before you even try. Ask someone else with approval rights to decide.
- **The variation came back with comments.** It goes back to the engineer who raised it; read the comment, fix the variation and submit it again.
- **The variation was rejected.** A rejection is final and does not reopen. Raise a new variation with its own number if the work is still needed.
- **"Returning or rejecting a variation needs a comment".** Write what has to change before you send the decision.
