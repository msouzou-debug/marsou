# Maintenance

The maintenance area is built in milestone M5 and is not available yet. The page says what it will hold and where the maintenance facts the system already keeps can be found today.

## Steps

1. Open «Συντήρηση» from the menu.
2. Read what the area will hold: work orders, preventive maintenance from the SLAs, backlog by risk band, execution on a phone.
3. Press one of the «Τι υπάρχει σήμερα» links to go to the asset register, the replacement forecast, a contract's defects, or the shutdowns and permits.

## What can go wrong

- **You expected a list of work orders.** There is none yet. Work orders arrive with milestone M5. Until then, a defect is recorded on the contract's «Ελλείψεις» tab and an asset's condition on the asset's own page.
