# Approvals

This screen gathers every approval waiting on you, grouped by type, with the facts you need to decide quickly. It opens one record at a time, without showing the whole file.

## Steps

1. Read the list grouped into Shutdowns, Variations, Certificates and Other, newest first.
2. Open a row to see its three key facts.
3. Press "Approve" or "Return with comments"; returning requires a comment.
4. Use ↑/↓ to move, a to approve, r to return, f for the next one and x to close; press ? to see every shortcut.

## What can go wrong

- **A record shows no decision buttons.** Some types only open their full record with "Open"; the decision happens there.
- **You pressed "Return with comments" with no comment.** The button only enables once you write one.
- **You arrived from a phone notification.** The record opens directly; approving takes only two taps.
