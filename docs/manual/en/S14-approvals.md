# Approvals

This screen gathers every approval waiting on you, grouped by type, with the facts you need to decide quickly. It opens one record at a time, without showing the whole file.

## Steps

1. Read the list grouped into Shutdowns, Variations, Certificates and Other, newest first.
2. Open a row to see its three key facts.
3. Press "Approve" or "Return with comments"; returning requires a comment. Once decided, the record leaves the list.
4. "Return with comments" sends the request back to the requester as a draft. They see it in their own Approvals marked "Returned for correction", correct it with "Edit" and submit it again.
5. As the clinical owner of an area you also see every "Area handback for acceptance" under Shutdowns: the works side says the works are finished. Press "Open", check the area, and on the permit press "Accept and close" or "Return with comments". A handback you return reaches whoever submitted it as "Area handback returned", with your comment.
6. Use ↑/↓ to move, a to approve, r to return, f for the next one and x to close; press ? to see every shortcut.

## What can go wrong

- **A record shows no decision buttons.** Some types only open their full record with "Open"; the decision happens there. An area handback is always accepted on the permit, where the handback checklist is.
- **You pressed "Return with comments" with no comment.** The button only enables once you write one.
- **The decision was not recorded.** The panel stays open and gives the reason in red, for example that the line is waiting on someone else or that your role cannot take this decision.
- **The record says no approver is appointed.** The unit has no active person with the role the line needs. An administrator sees it, and decides it or appoints an approver in User management. When the administrator decides it, the line carries the administrator's name. An administrator does not decide a line that is waiting on someone else.
- **A line you were waiting on left your list, or a new one appeared.** When an administrator switches an account off or takes a role away from it, the lines waiting on that person move to the next approver in the unit, or wait with no approver if there is nobody else. The move is recorded in the audit trail under the administrator's name.
- **You arrived from a phone notification.** The record opens directly; approving takes only two taps.
