# New asset / Edit asset

This form records or edits one asset's details: identity, source and cost, life cycle and criticality.

## Steps

1. Fill in the name and asset class, and pick its area from the unit's own area list.
2. If the asset belongs to another asset (for example a pump inside a chiller), search for it by tag or name.
3. If the asset is a system that feeds other areas, pick the system and mark the areas it serves.
4. Record the manufacturer, model, serial number and the installed, commissioned and warranty-end dates.
5. Pick the source project and contract, if any, and the acquisition cost.
6. Record the expected life, the replacement year and the replacement cost estimate.
7. Pick the asset's criticality (1 to 5) and press "Save".

## What can go wrong

- **You see the form without being able to edit it.** A technician sees the asset's details without editing rights; condition is recorded from the asset's own page.
- **You cannot find the source project or contract.** The list only shows projects and contracts from the same unit as the asset.
- **You do not see the areas the asset serves.** That field only shows once a system is picked.
