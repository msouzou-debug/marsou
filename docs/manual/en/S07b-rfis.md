# RFIs

This screen records the questions raised with the contractor or the designer on a contract, who they went to, when, and their deadlines. Project engineers and estates staff use it to record a question, attach the correspondence, record the answer and close it.

eCapital does not send e-mail by itself: there is no outgoing mail server on the SHSO network yet. "Send by e-mail" opens Outlook with the message ready; you send it, and record here the day you sent it and the message itself.

## Steps

1. Press "Add", write the question and the recipient (name and organisation). If the contract's contractor has contact details in the contractor register, the recipient and the e-mail address are filled in from them; you can change both, and the change does not go back to the register. The recipient's e-mail address is optional, but "Send by e-mail" does not work without it. The deadline is 7 days unless you set a different one.
   In "Letter or e-mail of the RFI" you can pick one or more files (.msg, .eml or .pdf, up to 50 MB each), for example the RFI letter. Then press:
   - "Save": the RFI is recorded and the files are attached under its "Correspondence".
   - "Save and send e-mail": today is also recorded as the sent date, and the file RFI-number.eml downloads with the message and the attachments. Open it (double-click): Outlook opens it as a new message from your own mailbox. Check it and press "Send" in Outlook. The button is disabled, and says so under it, until the recipient's e-mail is filled in.
2. Open the RFI and press "Send by e-mail". Outlook opens with the RFI's address as the recipient, the subject "Request for information number — contract — start of the question", and a message with the question, the reply deadline, the contract, the project and a line asking the recipient to quote the RFI number in the reply. A very long question is cut short in the message with "…"; complete it in Outlook if you need to. Check the message and send it.
3. Once Outlook has opened, eCapital reminds you to record the day it was sent. Press "Sent today" to record it in one click, or set another day with "Edit". If you sent the RFI some other way, fill in "Sent on" on the form, or later with "Edit".
4. Follow the recipient, the sent date and the deadline in the "Recipient", "Sent" and "Due" columns: green, amber, red or overdue.
5. Click a row, or select it and press Enter, to see the whole RFI: number, question, recipient, sent date, who raised it and when, due date, status, answer and correspondence.
6. Under "Correspondence", press "Attach a file" and pick the message you sent (saved from Outlook as .msg, or as .eml or .pdf). Attach the reply the same way when it arrives. Each file opens with "View" or is saved with "Download"; a .msg or .eml is saved and opens in Outlook.
7. To correct the question, the due date, the recipient or the sent date, press "Edit" and then "Save". You can do this until the RFI is closed, and the change is kept in the audit trail.
8. When the answer arrives, open the RFI and press "Answer". Write the answer and the "Answered on" date, the day you received it (today is suggested), and press "Save". The date cannot be later than today or before the day the RFI was raised. The deadline clock stops at the end of that day. In the optional "Contractor's letter or e-mail" pick the reply as you received it (.msg, .eml or .pdf); it is attached under "Correspondence" as "Contractor's reply".
9. To close the RFI, press "Close RFI", add a closing note if you wish, and press "Close RFI" again. An RFI can be closed without an answer when the question no longer matters; the closing note is then required and says why.
10. After each answer or close the list is refreshed and the RFI stays open on screen with a short confirmation. A closed RFI shows who closed it, when, and the note, and no longer offers "Edit".
11. To go back to the list, press "Back to the list" at the top right of the RFI, press Esc, or click outside the RFI, on the list.

## What can go wrong

- **"Send by e-mail" is greyed out.** The RFI has no recipient e-mail address. Add it with "Edit".
- **"The record was saved, but the files below were not attached."** The RFI (or the answer) was recorded; a file was refused, and the reason is given next to its name. The "Attach a file" form opens under "Correspondence"; attach it again from there once the problem is fixed.
- **The .eml file opened as a received message, not a new one.** It was opened by another program or by the new Outlook. Open it with classic Outlook (right-click, "Open with").
- **You pressed "Send by e-mail" and nothing opened.** The computer has no mail program set for e-mail links. Ask IT to make Outlook the default.
- **"Your session has expired. Sign in again."** A session lasts eight hours. Anything you typed and did not save is lost if you sign in from the same tab; to keep it, press "Sign in in a new tab", sign in, come back to the first tab and press "Save" again.
- **The deadline turned red or overdue.** This does not block anything: answer it and the clock stops there.
- **You do not see "Add", "Edit", "Answer", "Close RFI" or "Attach a file".** Only a project engineer, the head of estates and the administrator record, correct, answer, close and attach correspondence to RFIs; other roles can only read them. Offline, the buttons are not shown.
- **You do not see "Answer".** The RFI already has an answer or is closed. An answer is not replaced; raise a new RFI if you need another clarification.
- **"The day the answer arrived cannot be before the day the RFI was raised."** Pick the day it was raised or a later one, up to today.
- **"Fill in this field" under "Recipient".** Every new RFI needs a recipient. Older RFIs without one can still be corrected without it.
- **"The e-mail address is not valid."** Correct it or leave the field empty.
- **"The sent date cannot be before the day the RFI was raised" or "The date cannot be later than today".** The sent date runs from the day you recorded the RFI up to today. On a new RFI that means today.
- **"The due date cannot be before the date it was raised."** Pick the day it was raised or a later one.
- **"eArchive does not accept this kind of file."** Attach a .msg, .eml, PDF, Office document or image, up to 50 MB.
- **A file shows "Waiting for a protocol number".** The file has gone to eArchive and the protocol number appears once eArchive answers; you can already open it.
- **"This RFI is closed and can no longer be changed."** A closed RFI is not corrected. Raise a new one if you need another clarification. Correspondence that arrives after it closed can still be attached.
- **"This RFI has no answer. Write why it is being closed."** An RFI without an answer closes only with a closing note. Write the reason, for example that the matter was settled on site.
