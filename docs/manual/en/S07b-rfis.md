# RFIs

This screen records the questions raised with the contractor on a contract and their deadlines. Project engineers and estates staff use it to raise a question, record the answer and close it.

## Steps

1. Press "Add" and write the question. The deadline is 7 days unless you set a different one.
2. Watch the deadline in the "Due" column: green, amber, red or overdue.
3. Click a row, or select it and press Enter, to see the whole RFI: number, question, who raised it and when, due date, status and answer.
4. To correct the question or the due date, press "Edit" and then "Save". You can do this until the RFI is closed, and the change is kept in the audit trail.
5. Record the answer once the contractor gives it.
6. Once it is answered, press "Complete" to close it.

## What can go wrong

- **The deadline turned red or overdue.** This does not block anything — answer it and the clock stops there.
- **You do not see "Add", "Edit" or the answer form.** Only a project engineer, the head of estates and the administrator record and correct RFIs; other roles can only read them.
- **"The due date cannot be before the date it was raised."** Pick the day it was raised or a later one.
- **"This RFI is closed and can no longer be changed."** A closed RFI is not corrected. Raise a new one if you need another clarification.
- **"You close an RFI once it has been answered."** Record the answer first, then press "Complete".
