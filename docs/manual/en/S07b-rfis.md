# RFIs

This screen records the questions raised with the contractor or the designer on a contract, who they went to, when, and their deadlines. Project engineers and estates staff use it to record a question, attach the correspondence, record the answer and close it.

eCapital sends no e-mail. You send the RFI from Outlook and record here who you sent it to, the day you sent it and the message itself.

## Steps

1. Press "Add", write the question and the recipient (name and organisation). If the contract's contractor has contact details in the contractor register, the recipient and the e-mail address are filled in from them; you can change both, and the change does not go back to the register. The recipient's e-mail address is optional. The deadline is 7 days unless you set a different one.
2. Send the RFI from Outlook. If you sent it today, fill in "Sent on" on the form; otherwise fill it in later with "Edit".
3. Follow the recipient, the sent date and the deadline in the "Recipient", "Sent" and "Due" columns: green, amber, red or overdue.
4. Click a row, or select it and press Enter, to see the whole RFI: number, question, recipient, sent date, who raised it and when, due date, status, answer and correspondence.
5. Under "Correspondence", press "Attach a file" and pick the message you sent (saved from Outlook as .msg, or as .eml or .pdf). Attach the reply the same way when it arrives. Each file opens with "View" or is saved with "Download"; a .msg or .eml is saved and opens in Outlook.
6. To correct the question, the due date, the recipient or the sent date, press "Edit" and then "Save". You can do this until the RFI is closed, and the change is kept in the audit trail.
7. When the answer arrives, open the RFI and press "Answer". Write the answer and the "Answered on" date, the day you received it (today is suggested), and press "Save". The date cannot be later than today or before the day the RFI was raised. The deadline clock stops at the end of that day.
8. To close the RFI, press "Close RFI", add a closing note if you wish, and press "Close RFI" again. An RFI can be closed without an answer when the question no longer matters; the closing note is then required and says why.
9. After each answer or close the list is refreshed and the RFI stays open on screen with a short confirmation. A closed RFI shows who closed it, when, and the note, and no longer offers "Edit".

## What can go wrong

- **The deadline turned red or overdue.** This does not block anything: answer it and the clock stops there.
- **You do not see "Add", "Edit", "Answer", "Close RFI" or "Attach a file".** Only a project engineer, the head of estates and the administrator record, correct, answer, close and attach correspondence to RFIs; other roles can only read them. Offline, the buttons are not shown.
- **You do not see "Answer".** The RFI already has an answer or is closed. An answer is not replaced; raise a new RFI if you need another clarification.
- **"The day the answer arrived cannot be before the day the RFI was raised."** Pick the day it was raised or a later one, up to today.
- **"Fill in this field" under "Recipient".** Every new RFI needs a recipient. Older RFIs without one can still be corrected without it.
- **"The e-mail address is not valid."** Correct it or leave the field empty.
- **"The sent date cannot be before the day the RFI was raised" or "The date cannot be later than today".** The sent date runs from the day you recorded the RFI up to today. On a new RFI that means today.
- **"The due date cannot be before the date it was raised."** Pick the day it was raised or a later one.
- **"eArchive does not accept this kind of file."** Attach a .msg, .eml, PDF, Office document or image, up to 50 MB.
- **A file shows "Waiting for a protocol number".** The file has gone to eArchive and the protocol number appears once eArchive answers; you can already open it.
- **"This RFI is closed and can no longer be changed."** A closed RFI is not corrected. Raise a new one if you need another clarification. Correspondence that arrives after it closed can still be attached.
- **"This RFI has no answer. Write why it is being closed."** An RFI without an answer closes only with a closing note. Write the reason, for example that the matter was settled on site.
