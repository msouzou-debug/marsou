# Maintenance backlog

This screen lists the work the maintenance agreement does not cover, each with a risk band and a cost estimate, and the totals by unit and band, funded and unfunded. What stays unfunded is the basis for next year's capital programme.

## Steps

1. Read the totals at the top: for each unit and risk band, how many items, what they cost and how much of that is funded.
2. Filter by unit, risk band, kind or status. "Automatic drafts only" shows what the system drafted from repeated failures.
3. Press an item's title to open its sheet. There you change kind, band, cost and status, and see why it was drafted and from which orders.
4. To turn an open item into a project, press "To a project" and confirm. A project is created at the Idea phase and the item is marked as funded.
5. Press "Add" for a new item, or "Export to Excel" for the file.

## What can go wrong

- **You do not see "To a project".** Only the head of estates and the administrator do it, and only on an open item.
- **An automatic draft looks wrong.** Change its status to "Dropped". The order history stays, so it is clear why it was drafted.
- **The totals do not match the list.** The totals count open and funded items only; the list also shows done and dropped ones unless you filter them out.
