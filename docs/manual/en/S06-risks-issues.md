# Risks and issues

This screen shows a project's risk and issue registers: the likelihood × impact matrix, the full risk list and the issue list. Engineers and estates staff use it to track what could delay the project and who is handling it.

## Steps

1. Open the "Risks and issues" tab from the project page, or press "All risks" / "All issues" on the matching cards.
2. Read the matrix: each cell shows how many risks sit at that likelihood and impact combination, coloured by its risk band.
3. In the risk table, click a row to open its full details and edit them.
4. In the issue table, click a row the same way. An open issue past its due date shows in red.
5. Press "Add" above either table to record a new risk or a new issue.

## What can go wrong

- **"You do not have access to this page".** The project is not yours, or it does not exist — this screen does not tell the two apart. Ask the system administrator for access.
- **"The data did not load".** The API did not answer. Press "Try again".
- **A matrix cell is red.** Its likelihood × impact score is above 12; the risk count and the band are also spelled out in words for anyone reading the screen in black and white.
- **You cannot pick a risk owner from a list.** There is no user directory in the system yet — enter the owner's user ID directly.
- **An issue's due date is red.** The issue is still open and its due date has passed.
