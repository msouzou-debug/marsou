# Replacement forecast

This screen shows the estimated cost of replacing assets, by unit and year, based on each asset's own replacement year and cost estimate.

## Steps

1. Set the year range you want with the "From year" and "To year" fields.
2. Filter by unit if you want to see just one unit.
3. Read the estimated-cost-per-year chart and the table of assets, cost and critical assets by unit and year.
4. Press "Export to Excel" to download the figures.

## What can go wrong

- **You do not see this screen.** The replacement forecast is only available to the head of estates, finance, executive readers and the administrator.
- **The table is empty.** No asset has a planned replacement inside the year range you picked; widen the range.
