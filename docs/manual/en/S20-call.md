# Log a call

This form logs a fault the moment you are told about it and creates a corrective or statutory work order. The contract timers count from the time of the call, and the form shows the deadlines before you submit.

## Steps

1. Press "New call" on the work orders screen. The unit is already picked from the unit switcher.
2. Find the asset by its tag or name. If there is none, press "No asset".
3. Pick the contract system. Its band and its three times show next to it.
4. Write in one sentence what is wrong and, if needed, details and the area.
5. Check who reported it and the call time. If the call came earlier by phone, enter the real time.
6. Read the deadlines at the bottom and press "Submit". The new order opens.

## What can go wrong

- **No deadlines show.** You have not picked a system, or the unit has no active maintenance agreement. The call is still logged, without timers.
- **You cannot find the asset.** Try part of the tag. If it is not in the register, log the call with "No asset" and ask Technical Services to record it.
- **The form has no "Submit".** Your role does not log calls.
- **Do not write patient details.** The order says what broke and where, never who was in the room.
