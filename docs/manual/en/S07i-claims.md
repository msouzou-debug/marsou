# Claims

This screen records the claims the contractor has made against the Organisation and the Organisation's claims against the contractor, with the stage of each. Engineers and the head of estates use it to follow them, and finance uses it for contingent liabilities.

## Steps

1. Open a contract and press the "Claims" tab.
2. Under the title, check the open claim amounts, shown separately for the contractor and the Organisation. A claim is open from the stage "Notified" to "Court".
3. Press "Add" for a new claim. Choose "Contractor claim" or "Organisation claim", write a title and an amount, and choose a stage.
4. Optionally fill in the date notified, a legal advisor and notes. The date notified is the day the claim was received or sent; it cannot be after today or before the contract was awarded.
5. Under "Claim documents", choose what the document is (claim letter, reply or lawyer's letter) and the file, for example the e-mail you received. It is filed with eArchive when you press Save. For more documents, open the claim again with Edit and attach the next one.
6. Tick "Contingent liability" when finance has to take the claim into account in the notes to the financial statements.
7. Each claim's documents show in the "Documents" column; press one to open it.
8. Press "Edit" on a row to change the stage or anything else. A claim that is settled or withdrawn is no longer counted in the open amounts.

## What can go wrong

- **You do not see the "Add" or "Edit" button.** Only the project engineer, the head of estates and the system administrator see them.
- **The open amount leaves out a claim.** It is at the stage "Settled" or "Withdrawn".
- **The open-claims warning on the Overview does not go away.** It goes when every claim on the contract is "Settled" or "Withdrawn".
- **"The date cannot be later than today".** The claim has already been notified; enter the day it was.
- **The claim was saved but the document was not.** The message under the form gives the reason, usually a file type eArchive does not take or a file over 50 MB. Fix it and press Save again.
- **"Fill in this field".** The title and the amount are required. The amount cannot be negative.
