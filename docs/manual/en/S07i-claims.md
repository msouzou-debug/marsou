# Claims

This screen records the claims the contractor has made against the Organisation and the Organisation's claims against the contractor, with the stage of each. Engineers and the head of estates use it to follow them, and finance uses it for contingent liabilities.

## Steps

1. Open a contract and press the "Claims" tab.
2. Under the title, check the open claim amounts, shown separately for the contractor and the Organisation. A claim is open from the stage "Notified" to "Court".
3. Press "Add" for a new claim. Choose "Contractor claim" or "Organisation claim", write a title and an amount, and choose a stage.
4. Optionally fill in the date notified, a legal advisor and notes.
5. Tick "Contingent liability" when finance has to take the claim into account in the notes to the financial statements.
6. Press "Edit" on a row to change the stage or anything else. A claim that is settled or withdrawn is no longer counted in the open amounts.

## What can go wrong

- **You do not see the "Add" or "Edit" button.** Only the project engineer, the head of estates and the system administrator see them.
- **The open amount leaves out a claim.** It is at the stage "Settled" or "Withdrawn".
- **The open-claims warning on the Overview does not go away.** It goes when every claim on the contract is "Settled" or "Withdrawn".
- **"Fill in this field".** The title and the amount are required. The amount cannot be negative.
