# Payment certificates

The screen shows a contract's payment certificates: how much is certified, how much is held as retention and how much is paid net. Engineers who record them and Finance who move them toward payment both use it.

## Steps

1. Open the «Certificates» tab on the contract page.
2. Click «Add» for a new certificate: fill in the period, the work-done value (cumulative) and materials on site.
3. Open a certificate to see retention, previously certified and net payable, each on its own line.
4. An engineer other than the one who created it clicks «Engineer approval».
5. Finance records the SAP invoice reference and clicks «Received by finance».
6. Finance records the paid date and clicks «Mark as paid». If needed, they check «Release retention».

## What can go wrong

- **The «Engineer approval» button is disabled.** You cannot approve a certificate you created yourself — ask another engineer to approve it.
- **You see a warning on releasing retention.** The contract's defects liability period has not ended yet — the release is not blocked, it only informs you.
- **You cannot see «Add».** Only the project engineer, the head of estates and the administrator record certificates.
- **"Data did not load".** The API did not respond. Click "Try again".
