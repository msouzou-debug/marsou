# Shutdowns and permits

This screen lists every work permit for a system shutdown, with its status, ICRA class and pending approvals. It also opens each permit's own record, with its full facts, approvals and history.

## Steps

1. Filter the table by status, system or unit to find the permit you need.
2. Press "Add" to start a new shutdown request.
3. Open a row to see the permit's facts, affected areas, approvals and history.
4. From the record, press "Start work" once the planned window opens, or "Print" for the A4 sheet.
5. While the permit is a draft or has been returned with comments, press "Edit" to change the same fields as the request. After editing you complete the ICRA again and submit the permit; it keeps the same reference. Every change is recorded in the history.

## Closing the permit in two steps

A permit closes when the area is handed back to its clinical owner. The works side starts the handback and the clinical owner completes it.

1. **The works side** (project engineer, head of estates, administrator): when the work is done, press "Complete the works". Confirm all five items of the handback checklist, add a note if needed, and press "Submit for acceptance". Who submitted it, when, and the time the works ended are recorded. The permit stays in force and the record reads "Handback awaiting acceptance".
2. **The clinical owner of the area** (the area's ward manager, Nursing or Infection Control of the unit): sees the handback in their Approvals, opens the permit and checks the area. They press "Accept and close" to sign that they have taken the area back; the permit closes in their name. If something is not ready, they press "Return with comments" and write what needs putting right.
3. After a return, the permit stays in force (or in breach, if it already was). Whoever submitted the handback sees the comment on the record and in their Approvals, puts it right, and presses "Complete the works" again. Every submission and every return stays in the history.

While the handback waits for acceptance, the permit does not go into a work window breach: the works finished inside the window. If it is returned after the window has ended, it goes into breach.

## What can go wrong

- **The permit shows a work window breach.** Work carried on past the planned end. Notify the head of estates and close the permit as soon as you can.
- **"Start work" is greyed out.** The permit must be approved first, and work can only be started inside the approved work window. The reason is written under the button, with the window's dates. Once the window opens, the button wakes up by itself, without reloading the page. If the window has ended, the permit can no longer be started; raise a new request with new dates.
- **You do not see "Start work".** The permit has already started, has been closed or rejected, or your role does not start work.
- **You do not see "Edit".** A permit under approval changes only once the approver returns it with comments. An approved permit can no longer change; for any change, raise a new request.
- **An approval line says no approver is appointed.** The unit has nobody for that role. An administrator decides it instead, or appoints an approver in User management.
- **"Submit for acceptance" is greyed out.** Confirm all five items of the handback checklist.
- **You do not see "Complete the works".** The permit has not started, the handback has already been submitted and is waiting for acceptance, or your role does not run the works.
- **You do not see "Accept and close".** Only the clinical owner of the permit's areas accepts the handback. Whoever submitted it does not accept it, and neither does an administrator for being one. If the record reads "Handback awaiting acceptance", the permit is waiting for the clinical owner.
