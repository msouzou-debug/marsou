# Defects

This screen lists the handover defects on a contract, with a risk band, a cost and a due date inside the defects liability period. Project engineers and estates staff use it to record and track defects through to close.

## Steps

1. Press "Add" for a new defect. Its source is "Handover" and you pick a risk band from the list.
2. Open a row to change the description, cost, risk band or status.
3. If a capital project is paying for the defect, check "Funded" and pick the project.
4. Watch the due date: an open defect past its due date shows in red.
5. The total at the bottom shows the count and the cost per risk band.

## What can go wrong

- **"To mark a defect as funded, name the project paying for it."** A defect cannot be marked as funded without the project that will fund it. Pick the project before saving.
- **The due date is red.** The defect stayed open past the end of the defects liability period. The contractor is no longer on the hook, and the fix falls on OKYpY.
- **You do not see "Add", or you cannot change a row.** Only a project engineer, the head of estates and the administrator record handover defects on this screen.
