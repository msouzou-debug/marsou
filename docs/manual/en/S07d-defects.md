# Defects

This screen lists the handover defects on a contract, with a risk band, a cost and a due date inside the defects liability period. Project engineers and estates staff use it to record and track defects through to close.

## Steps

1. Press "Add" for a new defect. Its source is "Handover" and you pick a risk band from the list. The date found is today; if the defect was found earlier, change it.
2. Click a row, or select it and press Enter, to see the whole defect: description, source, area, risk band, cost, funding, status, who recorded it and when it was found, the due date and, once closed, who closed it and when.
3. To change the description, date found, cost, risk band or status, press "Edit" and then "Save". The change is kept in the audit trail.
4. If a capital project is paying for the defect, check "Funded" and pick the project.
5. Watch the due date: an open defect past its due date shows in red.
6. The total at the bottom shows the count and the cost per risk band.

## What can go wrong

- **"To mark a defect as funded, name the project paying for it."** A defect cannot be marked as funded without the project that will fund it. Pick the project before saving.
- **You do not see "Edit" on a closed defect.** A closed defect is not changed. Press "Reopen" first, then make the correction.
- **"The date cannot be later than today."** The date found is the day the defect was found. Pick today or an earlier day.
- **The due date is red.** The defect stayed open past the end of the defects liability period. The contractor is no longer on the hook, and the fix falls on OKYpY.
- **You do not see "Add" or "Edit".** Only a project engineer, the head of estates and the administrator record and change handover defects on this screen.
