# Asset labels

This page prints the QR labels of the assets you selected in the register, on an A4 sheet ready for label stock.

## Steps

1. Select the assets in the asset register and press "Print labels".
2. Check that every label shows the right tag, name and area.
3. Press "Print" to print the sheet.

## What can go wrong

- **The page says no assets are selected.** Go back to the asset register and select at least one asset before pressing "Print labels".
- **The label does not scan after printing.** Check that your printer is printing at full resolution and has not shrunk the page.
