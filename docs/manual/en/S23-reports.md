# Reports

This page gathers the seven management reports: capital programme by unit, project exceptions, contractor scorecard, maintenance backlog by risk band, asset lifecycle, clinical disruption and statutory compliance. Each report is worked out when you open it, from the same figures the other screens show.

## Steps

1. Open «Αναφορές» (Reports) from the menu.
2. Read the cards. Each one says what the report shows, who it is for and which filters it takes: unit, year or period.
3. Press the title of the report you want. The report page opens, with its filters and the "Download Excel" and "Print / PDF" buttons.

## What can go wrong

- **You do not see «Αναφορές» in the menu.** The reports are for the head of estates, finance, the executive, the auditor and the administrator. If you need them under another role, ask the system administrator for the role.
- **You see "You do not have access to this page".** Your role does not include the reports. Ask the system administrator for the role.
- **You want a report that is not on the list.** Write down what you need and send it to Technical Services.
