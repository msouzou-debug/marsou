# Run a work order

This screen is built for a phone, one-handed: it shows the asset, where it is, what to do and the checklist, with large buttons for the next step at the bottom. The camera button always sits above those buttons, wherever you are on the page.

## Steps

1. Open the order from the list and press "Run on a phone".
2. Read the asset name, the area and "What to do".
3. Press "Start" when you begin. If you stop, press "Pause" and then "Resume".
4. Tick the checklist as you go. The ticks stay on the screen only; write anything that must be kept in the completion note.
5. Press the camera for a photo of the fault or the work.
6. On a breakdown, press "Restored" as soon as the system works again.
7. Press "Complete", write what was done and, on a breakdown, pick the three codes.

## What can go wrong

- **You see "Offline" and the buttons are disabled.** The phone lost signal. In this version nothing is saved without a connection; move to a spot with signal and try again.
- **There is no "Restored".** It only shows on a breakdown in progress, not on a preventive order.
- **There are no buttons at all.** The order is closed, or your role does not move orders on.
