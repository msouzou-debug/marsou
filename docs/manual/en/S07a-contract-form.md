# New contract / Edit contract

This form records the contract awarded on a project, or changes an existing one's facts. Project engineers and estates staff use it right after an award.

## Steps

1. On the project page, press "Add" on the "Contracts" card for a new contract, or "Edit" on an existing one.
2. For a new contract, pick the contractor and fill in the contract number, type, award date and original value.
3. Fill in the remaining dates, the retention percentage, the performance bond and its expiry, the liquidated damages rate, the defects liability period and the SAP PO number.
4. Press "Save". The page takes you to the contract.

## What can go wrong

- **The contractor shows up disabled in the list.** It is blacklisted and takes no new contract; the reason shows above its name.
- **"The project has not been awarded yet".** The project must be at the award phase or later before you can record a contract on it.
- **You cannot change the contractor or the original value on an existing contract.** They cannot change once the contract exists; the current value follows automatically from the original value plus the approved variations.
- **"You do not have access for this action".** Only the project engineer, the head of estates and the system administrator record or change a contract.
