# Contractor scorecard

This screen shows how well the contractor kept the contract times over a period: response, restore, reports, preventive work, downtime hours and the penalties that follow. It is worked out each time from the orders themselves, so it always agrees with the list.

## Steps

1. Pick the maintenance agreement.
2. Pick a period: "This quarter", "Last quarter", or your own dates under "From" and "To". The agreement is paid quarterly.
3. Read the tiles. Under each percentage is how many were on time out of how many were due.
4. Read the table by priority band, the penalties by kind, the share of the penalty cap used and the assets with three or more failures.
5. Press "Export to Excel". The file carries the formulas behind the percentages and penalties, so finance can check the amount it withholds.

## What can go wrong

- **You see "rates incomplete".** The systems catalogue is missing penalty amounts. Late items were counted but not priced. Ask the head of estates to fill them in under Preventive maintenance.
- **A percentage shows "—".** Nothing was due for that timer in the period.
- **The share of the penalty cap is missing.** The agreement has no value recorded.
