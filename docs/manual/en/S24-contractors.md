# Contractors

This screen holds the register of contractors working with the organisation, shared across all twelve units. The system administrator and the head of estates manage it; engineers pick from it when they record a contract.

## Steps

1. Open «Διαχείριση» and choose the «Ανάδοχοι» tab. The first tab is «Χρήστες».
2. Press "Add" for a new contractor, or open a row to edit it.
3. Fill in the name, VAT number, registration number, category and SAP code, and press "Save".
4. Under "Contact details", fill in the contractor's contact person, e-mail and telephone. They fill in the recipient of a new RFI and show on the page of every contract the contractor holds. No bank details are recorded.
5. The system administrator also sees a "Blacklist" toggle when editing a contractor.

Once eFinance starts sending the e-mail and telephone of SAP's vendors, the daily sync will fill in the contact details of contractors that have an SAP code and an empty contact. Details a user typed are never replaced. Details that came from eFinance say so under the fields; if you change them, they become yours and eFinance no longer touches them.

## What can go wrong

- **"You do not have access to this page".** Only the system administrator and the head of estates manage the contractor register.
- **You do not see the "Blacklist" toggle.** Only the system administrator can blacklist a contractor or lift a blacklisting.
- **"The e-mail address is not valid" or "The telephone number is not valid".** Correct the field or leave it empty. A telephone is written with digits, spaces and the symbols + - ( ).
- **The contractor is blacklisted.** It takes no new contract; the ones it already holds carry on as they are.
