# Site instructions

This screen records the instructions given to the contractor on a contract and shows which ones carry a cost. Project engineers and estates staff use it to issue an instruction and turn it into a contract variation once it costs something.

## Steps

1. Press "Add", write the instruction, check the date issued and mark whether it has a cost impact. The date is today; if the instruction was given on site earlier, change it.
2. The "Cost impact" column shows at a glance which instructions cost something.
3. Click a row, or select it and press Enter, to see the whole instruction: number, text, who issued it and when, cost impact and variation.
4. To correct the text, the date issued or the cost impact, press "Edit" and then "Save". The change is kept in the audit trail.
5. For a cost-impact instruction with no variation yet, press "Create variation". This opens a draft variation on S08, priced at zero until the engineer works out the cost.
6. For an instruction that already has a variation, the link in the "Variation" column opens the variations list.
7. To go back to the list, press "Back to the list" at the top right of the instruction, press Esc, or click outside the instruction, on the list.

## What can go wrong

- **You do not see "Create variation".** Either the instruction has no cost impact, or it already has a variation — you cannot create a second variation from the same instruction.
- **You do not see "Edit" on an instruction.** It has already become a variation and can no longer be changed. Make the correction on the variation.
- **"The date cannot be later than today."** The date issued is the day the instruction was given. Pick today or an earlier day.
- **The amber strip at the top.** The contract has cost-impact instructions with no variation yet. Create one once you are ready to price it.
- **You do not see "Add".** Only a project engineer, the head of estates and the administrator issue site instructions.
