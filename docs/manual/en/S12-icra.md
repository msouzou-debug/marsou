# Infection control risk assessment (ICRA)

This wizard works out a shutdown's class of precautions, from the type of work and the risk group of the areas it affects. It shows the matrix cell that produced the class, so you can see why it came out that way.

## Steps

1. Choose the work type, A to D, based on each card's description.
2. Review the risk group of every affected area and optionally fill in the surrounding areas above, below, lateral, behind and in front.
3. Review the computed class and the matrix cell that produced it, and tick "Acknowledged" for every mandatory control.
4. Check the overview and press "Submit" to send the permit for approval.

## What can go wrong

- **"Submit" stays disabled.** You must tick "Acknowledged" for every control of the class first.
- **A message says Class II does not apply.** Class II is not allowed for construction or renovation work. Choose a different work type or review the areas.
- **The submission shows a clash warning.** Another permit overlaps in time or in area. The warning does not block the submission, it only tells you.
